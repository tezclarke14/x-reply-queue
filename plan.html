2026-10-22Ohm Quo calls, Oct 21–22 (thread: 11 callbacks)
1/ 11 calls in 2 days trying to get our new HVAC company onto property managers' vendor lists. Here's what I learned.

2/ Half of them assumed I wanted to rent an apartment or buy a house. Lead with "HVAC vendor" in the first five words, not the end.

3/ The big management companies don't pick vendors on the phone. One told me to get on NetVendor first. Once you're approved, you pop up when any of their properties searches "air conditioning."

4/ Warranties are a quiet competitor. One realtor said all her properties are under a home warranty, so she has no need for a tech right now.

5/ The best call wasn't a yes. A realtor said "I appreciate the hustle" and offered to post us in her 177-person vendor group chat.

6/ Cold calling is still the best way into the trades. Next step: turning these into the systems our AI will run.
